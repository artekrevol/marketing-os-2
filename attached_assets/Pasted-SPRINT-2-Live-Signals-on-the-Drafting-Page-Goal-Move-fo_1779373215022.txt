──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TASK 1 — Banned-phrase highlighter (ships first, smallest scope)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
File: artifacts/insight-forge/src/pages/DraftingInterface.tsx (+ helper)
Behavior:
  - As writer types, scan against the brand's banned_phrases list.
  - Each match highlighted inline. Hover tooltip shows the suggested
    replacement from voice_library.
  - One-click accept applies the replacement.
  - Use the freshly-sanitized voice_flags pipeline. Do NOT re-implement
    detection logic.
Acceptance:
  - Writer can type 'leverage our cutting-edge solutions', see live
    highlight, hover for replacement, click to apply.
  - No screen flicker on rapid typing.
  - No false positives from punctuation/whitespace boundaries.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TASK 2 — Live originality meter
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
File: DraftingInterface.tsx + new local-heuristic helper
Behavior:
  - Local heuristic on keystroke (debounce 300ms): sentence-length
    variance, n-gram repetition, transition-phrase density. Produces a
    quick proxy score.
  - Originality.ai call on 5s typing-pause debounce. Reconciles the
    local proxy.
  - Color bands: green <15%, yellow 15-20%, red >20%.
  - Render per chosen placement.
Acceptance:
  - Indicator updates within 500ms of typing pause.
  - On save, Originality.ai reconciles the local heuristic score.
  - Score persists across section navigation.
  - Non-blocking — no spinner gates writing.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TASK 3 — Atomic-question coverage tracker (biggest scope; plan carefully)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
File: DraftingInterface.tsx sidebar + new check at
      lib/worker/src/jobs/content/checks/atomic-coverage.ts
Behavior:
  - Sidebar lists every atomic_question from the locked brief.
  - Each is a checkbox auto-ticked when a paragraph in the draft
    answers it.
  - Verifier: cosine-sim between question embedding and paragraph
    embeddings (start threshold 0.65), followed by LLM verify pass
    on ambiguous matches (0.50-0.75 band).
  - Re-runs on section approve, not on every keystroke.
Acceptance:
  - Draft answering 10 of 12 brief questions shows 10/12 in sidebar
    with the 2 unanswered marked red.
  - Click an unanswered question → "no paragraph in your draft
    answers this" surface in the editor.
  - False-positive rate <10% audited on first 20 real drafts.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TASK 4 — Live voice-match per section (ships last; calibration-heavy)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
File: DraftingInterface.tsx + reuse runBrandVoiceCheck from Sprint 1
Behavior:
  - Each section header shows current voice-match score.
  - Score updates on section approve (not keystroke — too expensive).
  - Score below threshold (default 70 for TekRevol) surfaces a "make
    this sound more like us" assist button.
  - Assist re-runs the section through the AI with voice_library
    exemplars in the prompt.
Acceptance:
  - Per-section score visible on every approved section.
  - Score persists across navigation.
  - Re-assist button works one-click.
  - Calibration session scheduled with Rabia after first 5 drafts
    have used it.
