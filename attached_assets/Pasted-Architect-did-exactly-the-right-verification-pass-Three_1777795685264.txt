The Sprint 2 queue rename never happened — that's actually a documentation bug in `replit.md` from when I drafted Sprint 3 against a hypothetical state. Architect caught it. Better to add the queue with intent now than retrofit a rename later.

Future sprints (Decision Engine nightly recompute in Sprint 7, ranking puller jobs, GA4 ingestion) all want a "scoring/batch" queue. Adding it now means three later sprints don't each have to make this decision.

The operational cost is one extra Worker registration in `lib/worker/src/index.ts` and one extra Queue export from `lib/jobs/queues.ts`. Trivial.
