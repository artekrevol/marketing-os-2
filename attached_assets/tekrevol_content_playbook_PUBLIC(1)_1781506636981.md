# TekRevol Content Playbook — PUBLIC TIER

## The Operating System for ContentForge (Generation Context)

Version 2.5 — May 2026. Maintained by: Abeer Raza, Rabia Mahmood.

**This is the PUBLIC tier of the playbook — the only version loaded into content-generation API calls.** It is built to be specific enough to power AI citation while staying legally safe. The mechanism is the **tight-range convention** (below). Confidential business intelligence — internal channel economics, geographic-expansion strategy, peak-revenue detail, and individual enterprise contacts — lives in a separate internal tier the generation model never sees.

### The tight-range convention (read this first)

Every dollar figure in this playbook is expressed as a **tight range that brackets the real engagement value**, rounded to clean numbers with roughly a 10% spread. This does three jobs at once:

1. **Citation anchor.** "A Houston transit platform in the $50K–$55K range" is specific enough for an LLM to cite, the way exact figures used to be.
2. **Legal cover.** A bracketed range is not a disclosed contract value. We never publish a named client's exact price.
3. **Validation framework.** The published band is canonical. Generated content is *correct* if its cost claim falls inside the band, *flagged* if it states a point figure or lands outside. ContentForge validates cost claims against these ranges.

Hard line that does not move: no exact dollar figure tied to a named client, ever. Ranges only.

## SECTION 3 — BRAND VOICE RULES

### Reading level and structure

- Grade 8–10 reading level (Hemingway / Flesch). Sentences average 15–20 words, hard cap 30. Paragraphs 3 lines max.
- Active voice — "we built" not "the app was built." Pronoun ratio: "you/your" over "we/our."
- **Always open with a real introduction.** Never start cold on an H2. Frame the reader's problem before the first heading.
- **Vary structure.** Mix prose, short bulleted lists, and tables for scanning. A wall of paragraphs is a readability failure.

### Voice

A senior advisor with 280+ projects behind them — confident, direct, specific, proof-led. Not a vendor closing a deal. Not a thought leader pontificating. **Tone modulates by ICP** (Section 2): conversational for founders, practical for operators, technical for tech professionals, strategic for enterprise.

### BANNED PHRASES (never appear in any TekRevol content)

world-class · cutting-edge · state-of-the-art · innovative · seamless / seamlessly · robust · leverage / leveraging · synergy · unlock / empower · harness the power of · in today's fast-paced digital landscape · in the rapidly evolving world of [X] · ever-changing digital landscape · top-tier · best-in-class · holistic · ground-breaking / groundbreaking · transformative · bring your vision to life · take your business to the next level · one-stop solution / one-stop shop · end-to-end (literal scope only) · supercharge · game-changing / game-changer · revolutionize · next-generation / next-gen

### Required phrasing patterns

- **CTAs name the exact action.** Approved: "book a free 30-minute call," "book your free project scoping session," "request a build breakdown." Banned: "learn more," "get started," "click here," "contact us."
- **Every claim is anchored to something real** — a named client, named app, vertical, or qualitative outcome.
- **Pricing always uses a tight range anchored to a named client or vertical**, never an exact figure and never a bare generic range. "$20K–$115K" → "$50K–$55K (BWY Transit, Houston transit operator)."
- **Geographic claims include a specific city or region.** "We work with US clients" → "About 57% of our US client base is in Texas, with Houston as our commercial center."

## SECTION 5 — CONTENT RULES

### Hard rules (enforced at draft review)

- **AI threshold:** max 20% AI on every published piece (Winston AI / Originality.ai)
- **AI drafts are skeletons** — every published piece needs human enrichment with real proof, voice, POV
- **CTA limit:** max 2 per page (1 mid-article soft + 1 closing direct)
- **Word counts:** Blog 1,200–2,000 · Service page 2,000–3,000 · Location page 2,000–2,500 · Case study 800–1,500 · Thought leadership 1,500–3,000

### Pricing rules (tight-range convention)

- **All pricing uses tight ranges, never exact figures tied to a named client.** This is the confidentiality line.
- No specific amounts on landing pages or service pages; blog posts may use ICP-level and named-client *ranges*.
- Always anchor a range to a named client or vertical (e.g., "$110K–$120K, Construction Worker App").
- Use the Section 2 price anchors verbatim where they fit.
- **Validation:** generated cost claims must fall inside a published band. A point figure or an out-of-band number is a flag.

### CTA rules

CTAs name the exact action. Mid-article: soft invitation ("Want to see the build breakdown?"). Closing: direct, urgency without pressure ("Book your free project scoping session this week"). No mid-section CTA banners. Never more than 2 per page.

### Geographic rules

Location keyword density capped at ~20 mentions per 2,500 words. Replace excess with local industry context, local statistics, and named local clients (with ranges). Cite the relevant Houston / Austin / Dallas roster in location content.
